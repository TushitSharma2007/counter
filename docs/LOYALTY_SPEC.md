# Loyalty: Product & Technical Spec

This is the source of truth for the loyalty product (Imprint). It works **inside the POS** and also **as a standalone product** for cafés that don't use our POS. Read `CLAUDE.md` first for the stack, tenancy and design rules.

---

## 1. The experience in one paragraph

A customer buys a coffee and sees a small, beautiful **stamp object on the counter** with "Tap your phone to collect your stamp". They tap. Their phone opens the café's loyalty page. The first time, they sign in with Google or email in about 10 seconds. Then an ink stamp **thumps down onto their digital card** and it says *"4 of 9 — 5 more for a free Cold Coffee."* Next visit they just tap, and it stamps instantly. If their phone has no NFC, they scan the QR next to the stamp instead. Nobody at the counter has to do anything, except confirm when a reward is redeemed.

## 2. Physical setup per outlet

| Item | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **NFC stamp** (stamp-shaped object with an NTAG 424 DNA tag inside) | Primary way to collect a stamp. See `NFC_STAMP_HARDWARE.md`. |

| *Optional:* **counter display** (spare phone/tablet at `/display/:outletId`) | Shows the rotating QR plus a live "Stamped: Riya" ticker (with a check icon) so staff see who stamped. |
| Small table tent / sticker | "Tap or scan to collect stamps · Free coffee on your 10th". It also explains where to tap: "tap the top of your iPhone / the back-middle of your Android". |

## 3. URLs and routing (`apps/loyalty`)

| Route | What it does |
|---|---|
| `/s/:stampPointId?picc=…&cmac=…` | **NFC tap entry.** Its query params are generated by the NTAG 424 SUN feature on every tap. |
| `/s/:stampPointId?t=…&m=…` | NFC prototype entry (NTAG213/215: static token + combined UID/counter mirror, §5.1). `security_level = basic`. |
| `/q/:token` | **QR scan entry.** The token is either a rotating signed token or a static QR id. |

`/s/` and `/q/` links are always served from the dedicated **stamp link domain** (`packages/config/brand.ts`, placeholder until bought). It is written into tags and must never change; it only redirects into the card app, so the main brand domain can change later.
| `/c/:merchantSlug` | The customer's card for that merchant (home). |
| `/c/:merchantSlug/rewards/:rewardId` | Redeem screen (one-time code + QR for the cashier). |
| `/login` | Google / email OTP (phone OTP when enabled). Keeps the pending `stampIntent` id in the URL. |
| `/welcome` | First-time profile: name, phone (required, not verified until phone OTP is on), birthday (optional), marketing consent. |
| `/display/:outletId` | Counter display: rotating QR + live stamp ticker. Paired once by staff (§5.2); holds a revocable per-display secret. |
| `/merchant/*` | Standalone merchant console (also embedded in the POS back office). |

All entry routes (`/s`, `/q`) do the **same thing** server-side: they call `POST /loyalty-stamp-intent` → get back `{ intentId, merchant, outlet, method }` → if the customer is logged in, call `POST /loyalty-stamp-claim` immediately; if not, go to `/login?intent=…` and claim after login.

## 4. Customer auth

- **Supabase Auth.** Providers:
  - **Google OAuth.** Free; the default button and fastest path.
  - **Email OTP** (6-digit code). Free, but configure a custom SMTP: Supabase's built-in mailer is heavily rate-limited. Use Resend's or Brevo's free tier.
  - **Phone OTP.** Built behind `CustomerAuthAdapter` but **disabled** by a feature flag. It needs a paid SMS provider (MSG91/Twilio) and **DLT registration** for Indian SMS (sender ID + template approval takes days, so start that early once we decide to pay). A WhatsApp OTP adapter is an alternative.
- One customer account works across **all merchants** on the platform. Each merchant gets its own `card`, and merchants can only see their own cardholders.
- Sessions last about a year with silent refresh. Ask for a login again only if the session is lost.
- **Session storage:** a server-set **httpOnly cookie** on our own domain, via a Cloudflare Pages Function that proxies Supabase auth (login, refresh, logout). Not `localStorage`: Safari deletes script-written storage after 7 days without a visit, and an NFC tap on iPhone opens Safari, not the installed PWA, so the two must share a cookie-based session.
- **Web Push on iOS** works only inside the installed PWA. It is a bonus channel, never the only way to reach a customer.
- **Login screen design:** the merchant's logo and card preview sit on top ("Join Bean Theory Rewards"), two big buttons (Google, Email) and a one-line privacy note. No clutter.
- Stamp intents survive the login round-trip: they're stored server-side and expire after 5 minutes.

## 5. Stamping methods & verification

### 5.1 NFC stamp
**Production: NTAG 424 DNA with SUN (Secure Unique NFC / SDM)**
- The tag is configured to write a URL like
  `https://<domain>/s/<stampPointId>?picc=<encrypted UID+counter>&cmac=<8-byte CMAC>`
  where `picc` and `cmac` **change on every tap**.
- Server (`loyalty-stamp-intent` edge function):
  1. Load the stamp point and its AES keys (`loyalty.tag_keys`, encrypted at rest; only the service role reads them).
  2. Decrypt `picc` (AES-128 with the SDM meta-read key) → tag UID + 3-byte tap counter.
  3. Check the UID matches the registered tag.
  4. Verify `cmac` with the SDM file-read key (AES-CMAC per the NXP AN12196 application note).
  5. Require `counter > last_counter` for that tag, then store the new counter. **This blocks replayed or copied URLs.**
  6. Create the stamp intent.
- Use a small, well-tested TS implementation (Web Crypto AES + a CMAC helper). Unit-test it against the **NXP AN12196 test vectors**.

**Prototype: NTAG213/215**
- The tag holds the URL `https://<stamp-domain>/s/<stampPointId>?t=<random token>&m=00000000000000x000000`. Enable the NTAG21x **ASCII mirror** of the UID and the **NFC read counter** together (`MIRROR_CONF = 11b`; configured in the tag's config pages; `tools/nfc-provisioner` `write_basic` does this). With both enabled, the tag writes them as **one contiguous block**: 14 hex chars of UID, an `x`, then 6 hex chars of counter (e.g. `m=04A1B2C3D4E5F6x00002A`). It cannot fill two separate params. Every real tap now produces a different URL.
- NTAG213 has only 144 bytes of user memory, which is why the stamp domain must be short.
- The server stores `last_counter` per stamp point and **rejects any counter ≤ last_counter** ("already used"). It also checks that the UID matches the registered tag.
- The counter isn't cryptographically signed, so a determined user could edit it. That's why the stamp point stays `security_level = 'basic'` and the stricter limits from §6 still apply (pending approval or bill match).

### 5.1a Single-use links (applies to every method)
Reopening a stamp link from browser history, refreshing, pressing back, or opening the same link on another device must **never** create another stamp.
1. **Server:** every NFC counter, rotating QR token and stamp intent is **single-use**. A second claim returns `ALREADY_USED`. This is the real protection; the client steps below are only for UX.
2. **Client:** on landing on `/s/…` or `/q/…`, immediately `history.replaceState` to `/c/:merchantSlug?intent=<id>`, then clear the `intent` param after the claim resolves. The stamp URL never remains in history, and a refresh shows only the card.
3. **Intent claims are idempotent:** claiming the same intent twice (double-tap, refresh mid-request) returns the original result. It never creates a second stamp.
4. **Cooldown + daily cap** (§6) back everything up.
5. **UX:** for `ALREADY_USED`, show "This stamp was already collected. Tap the stamp at the counter for your next one." Never show a raw error.
6. **Tests (Playwright):** claim → reload → back → reopen the original URL → open it in a second logged-in session. The stamp count must increase **exactly once**.

**Phone support notes (show these in the help sheet):**
- iPhone XS and newer read NFC URLs in the background when the screen is on and unlocked. The antenna is at the **top edge**.
- Android: NFC must be **on** (many budget phones ship with it off), and the antenna is usually **mid-back**. Some budget phones have no NFC at all, which is why there's always a QR.

### 5.2 QR code (two merchant-selectable modes)
- **Rotating QR (recommended):** `/display/:outletId` shows a QR of `/q/<token>`, where `token = base64url({stampPointId, step}) + truncated HMAC-SHA256(displaySecret, stampPointId ‖ step)` and `step = floor(unixTime / 30)`. The display generates tokens **locally** (TOTP-style); there is no polling. The server accepts the current step and one step either side (≈60 s validity, absorbs clock drift). Each `(stampPointId, step)` can be claimed **once per customer**. Screenshots and forwarded photos expire.
  - **Pairing:** staff pair a display once from the POS/merchant console (`loyalty-display-pair`). The server creates a random per-display secret, stores it encrypted in `loyalty.tag_keys.display_secret_enc`, and hands it to that display once. Re-pairing or "Unpair" revokes it.
  - This is a deliberate, scoped exception to "no secrets in the client": the secret lives only on a staff-paired counter device and can only mint stamp tokens for one stamp point.
- **Static printed QR:** `/q/<staticId>`. Stamps from it are created as `status = 'pending'`, and staff approve them with one tap in the POS/merchant console (a realtime list with the customer's name and photo initial). **Or**, in integrated mode, a static-QR stamp auto-approves only if a POS bill for that customer exists in the last N minutes (attached by phone number).

### 5.3 Stamp modes (per merchant setting: `stamp_mode`)

| Mode | Use when | How a stamp gets approved |
|---|---|---|
| `bill_match` | **Pilot** with basic tags, café bills on our POS | Auto-approved if an unstamped POS bill exists for this customer's phone at this outlet in the last 15 min (configurable). One bill = one stamp. |
| `staff_approval` | **Pilot** with basic tags, no POS / other POS | Stamp is `pending`; the counter device shows it live (name + "tapped just now" + Approve/Reject + soft sound). Approve takes 1 tap. Customer's screen updates by itself via realtime. Expires after 10 min; staff can still approve from "Missed" until day close. |
| `automatic` | After moving to **NTAG 424 DNA** (or the rotating QR) | Approved instantly; the tap/scan itself proves presence. |

Rules:
- A basic tag or a static QR can **never** use `automatic`. The server enforces this even if the setting says otherwise.
- **Bill match, customer side:** if no bill matches, show "We couldn't find your bill. Ask the cashier to add your number." Keep the intent for 15 min; when staff attach the number to the bill, the stamp completes and the customer's open page animates.
- **Bill match, POS side:** the billing screen has a prominent "Loyalty number" field (with a `/` shortcut and recent customers). The cashier sees the **cardholder's name** before confirming. The bill shows "Stamp pending/collected".
- **Bill match, phone trust:** phone numbers are not verified (phone OTP is off), so one phone number can belong to only **one cardholder per merchant** (enforced when a card is created or a phone is changed). Phone changes are logged and shown in "Suspicious activity".
- **Staff approval UI:** works on the merchant console, the POS and `/display/:outletId`. Approve/Reject buttons are ≥ 56px. Show a running count ("3 waiting").
- The customer's **pending screen** shows their card with a soft "Waiting for the counter…" state, not a spinner on a blank page. When approved, the stamp animation plays.

## 6. Anti-fraud rules (server-side, configurable per merchant)

| Rule | Default |
|---|---|
| Login required to claim | Always |
| Cooldown per customer per outlet | 2 hours |
| Max stamps per customer per day (all outlets; counts **stamps**, not visits) | 2 |
| NFC 424 counter must increase | Always |
| Rotating QR token single-use per customer, 60 s expiry | Always |
| Static QR / basic NFC → `bill_match` or `staff_approval` only (never `automatic`) | Always |
| Tap-counter must increase (basic NTAG215 with counter mirror) | Always |
| Stamp intent expiry | 5 min |
| Rate limit per IP / device | 10 claims / hour |

Every claim attempt, successful or not, is written to `loyalty.events` with method, outlet, IP, user agent and result. The merchant console has a **"Suspicious activity"** view (many failed claims, unusual times, the same device on many accounts).

When a claim is rejected, the customer sees a friendly reason ("You've already collected a stamp here in the last 2 hours. Come back later!"). The text is friendly; it never shows a raw error code. No emoji anywhere in customer copy.

## 7. Rules engine (`packages/loyalty-core`)

A pure function, with no I/O:

```ts
evaluate(program: Program, card: CardState, event: StampEvent): Evaluation
// → { stampsAdded, pointsAdded, rewardsUnlocked: RewardGrant[], messages: string[], rejected?: Reason }
```

`Program` (Zod-validated JSON on `loyalty.programs.rules`) supports:
- `type`: `'stamps' | 'points' | 'hybrid'`
- `tiers`: `[{ stamps: 5, reward: {...} }, { stamps: 10, reward: {...}, resetsCard: true }]`. On reset, stamps above the final threshold **carry over** to the new card (e.g. 9 + a double stamp on a 10-card → reward + 1 stamp on the new card).
- `earn`: `{ per: 'visit' }` (self-stamping is always per visit; in integrated mode the POS can also send `{ per: 'item', itemIds, categoryIds }` or `{ minBillPaise }`)
- `limits`: `{ cooldownMinutes, maxPerDay, participatingOutletIds }`. `maxPerDay` caps **stamps** (a multi-stamp event is trimmed to what's left of the cap).
- `rewards`: `free_item(itemId|name) | percent_off | flat_off | bogo`, each with `validDays`
- `expiry`: `{ stampsExpireAfterInactiveDays? }`
- `bonuses`: `welcomeStamp`, `birthdayReward`, `happyHours: [{ days, from, to, multiplier }]`, `referral`
- `design`: `{ cardName, logoUrl, bgColor, inkColor, stampIcon, slotsShape }`

Merchants edit all of this through a **friendly form with a live card preview**, never a JSON editor. Ship 3 starter templates: "Classic 9+1 coffee", "5 & 10 tiers", "Spend points".

Test cases (minimum): tier unlock exactly at the threshold; card reset after the final tier, with carry-over; cooldown boundary; happy-hour multiplier; daily stamp cap trimming a multi-stamp event; expiry; welcome stamp only once; participating outlets.

## 8. Data model (`loyalty` schema)

```
merchants        id, org_id NOT NULL (standalone merchants get an org with POS modules off), name, slug, plan, settings jsonb, created_at
outlets          id, merchant_id, pos_outlet_id NULL, name, address, lat, lng
customers        id (= auth.users.id), name, phone, email, birthday, marketing_opt_in, created_at
programs         id, merchant_id, version, rules jsonb, is_active, created_at
cards            id, merchant_id, customer_id, program_id, stamps int, points int, lifetime_stamps,
                 last_stamp_at, created_at   UNIQUE(merchant_id, customer_id)
stamp_points     id, merchant_id, outlet_id, kind ('nfc_424'|'nfc_basic'|'qr_rotating'|'qr_static'),
                 label ("Counter stamp"), tag_uid NULL, last_counter int NULL, static_token NULL,
                 security_level ('secure'|'basic'), is_active
tag_keys         stamp_point_id PK, meta_read_key_enc NULL, file_read_key_enc NULL,
                 display_secret_enc NULL, display_paired_at NULL   -- service role only, no RLS read
stamp_intents    id, stamp_point_id, method, created_at, expires_at, claimed_by NULL, claimed_at NULL, ip, ua
stamps           id, card_id, outlet_id, stamp_point_id, method, status ('approved'|'pending'|'rejected'),
                 count int, approved_by NULL, pos_order_id NULL, created_at
rewards          id, card_id, tier_index, reward jsonb, status ('available'|'redeemed'|'expired'),
                 unlocked_at, expires_at, redeemed_at NULL, redeemed_outlet_id NULL, redeemed_by NULL,
                 redeem_code (6 chars, unique per merchant while active)
events           id, merchant_id, type, payload jsonb, created_at   -- audit + webhooks + analytics
push_subscriptions  id, customer_id, merchant_id, endpoint, keys jsonb
```

RLS:
- A customer reads only their own `customers` row, `cards`, `stamps` and `rewards`.
- Merchant staff read only their merchant's rows.
- `tag_keys` and `stamp_intents` are **never** readable from the client.
- All writes to `stamps`, `cards` and `rewards` happen **only** in edge functions/RPCs (`security definer`), never directly from the client.

Claiming a stamp is **one Postgres transaction** (an RPC called by the edge function): lock the card row → check limits → insert the stamp → update the counters → insert any unlocked rewards → insert the event.

## 9. API (Supabase Edge Functions)

| Function | Caller | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `loyalty-stamp-intent` | public | Verify NFC SUN / QR token → create an intent |
| `loyalty-stamp-claim` | customer | Claim an intent → run `evaluate` → return `{ card, animation: { slotIndex, stampsAdded }, rewardsUnlocked }` |
| `loyalty-display-pair` | staff | Pair (or re-pair/unpair) a counter display; returns its per-display secret once. Tokens are then generated on the display (§5.2). |
| `loyalty-stamp-approve` | staff | Approve/reject pending stamps |
| `loyalty-redeem` | staff | Confirm a reward by code/QR, atomically |
| `loyalty-pos-attach` | POS | (Integrated) attach a customer to a bill; auto-approve matching pending stamps; award item-based stamps |
| `loyalty-provision-tag` | owner | Register a stamp point and store the encrypted keys for an NTAG 424 |

Realtime channels: `loyalty:outlet:<id>` (new and pending stamps for the counter display and console) and `loyalty:card:<id>` (the customer's card updates live if they keep the page open).

## 10. Build order

1. `loyalty-core` rules engine + tests.
2. Schema + RLS + seed (a demo merchant, 2 outlets, a "Classic 9+1" program).
3. Customer auth (Google + email OTP with free SMTP) + `/welcome` + the card page.
4. **Rotating QR + counter display + claim flow + stamp animation + single-use links (§5.1a).** Demo-able with zero hardware.
5. NFC pilot with NTAG215 (`security_level = basic`, UID + counter mirror) + the `staff_approval` and `bill_match` modes (§5.3). Until the merchant console exists (step 8), stamp points are created with a temporary CLI (`pnpm loyalty:stamp-point create --outlet <id> --kind nfc_basic --label "Counter stamp"`) or the seed.
6. NTAG 424 DNA SUN verification (test vectors first, then a real tag) + the tag provisioning screen.
7. Rewards + redemption screen + staff confirm.
8. Merchant console: live feed, approvals, customers, program editor with live preview, stamp points, suspicious activity.
9. POS integration (`loyalty-pos-attach`, reward as a discount line).
10. Web Push: "You're 1 stamp away", "Your reward expires in 3 days", and simple campaigns.

## 11. Screens (customer, phone-first)

1. **Landing / login.** Merchant brand on top, card preview, Google + Email buttons.
2. **Welcome.** Name, phone, birthday (optional), "Get offers" opt-in.
3. **Stamp moment.** Full-screen card, stamp animation (see `CLAUDE.md` §6.5), progress text, "View card" button.
4. **Card home.** The card with stamp slots, progress, available rewards, a "How to collect" help sheet, outlet list, and an "Add to Home Screen" nudge (shown once).
5. **Reward detail / redeem.** A big 6-char code + QR, "Show this to the cashier", a countdown once opened (5 min).
6. **Error states.** Cooldown, expired QR ("Scan the QR on the counter again"), tag error, offline.

Merchant console and counter display follow the POS design system.
